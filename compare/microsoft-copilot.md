# IOMI vs Microsoft Copilot

> Markdown mirror of https://www.iomifinance.com/compare/microsoft-copilot. Last updated: 2026-08-24.

Copilot lives where your spreadsheet lives. That's exactly the problem.

**The short version:** Microsoft Copilot makes the manual workflow faster — formulas written for you, tables cleaned, charts on demand. IOMI removes the manual workflow: accounts sync themselves, every asset class is native, and the picture comes with a score and a next move.

## The comparison

| | IOMI | Microsoft Copilot |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A wealth platform that replaces the finance spreadsheet entirely | An AI assistant inside Windows and Microsoft 365 — including Excel |
| Live accounts | Read-only sync with 12,000+ institutions via Plaid; balances update themselves | None — your spreadsheet is as fresh as your last manual update |
| Asset coverage | Native: accounts, investments, crypto, real estate with live valuations, private equity, collectibles, vehicles, liabilities | Whatever you type into the sheet |
| Business + personal | Up to 3 business entities alongside personal wealth — separated, attributed, consolidated | Separate tabs, maintained by hand |
| Intelligence layer | Capital Efficiency Score, Next Dollar Allocation, Goals, Simulator — on live data | Formula help and summaries of what you built |
| The math | Computed by a deterministic engine; the AI phrases the numbers, it never invents them | Your formulas, your errors — now written faster |

Microsoft Copilot capabilities as published by Microsoft, August 2026.

## Why IOMI

- **Live beats fast.** A faster way to update a stale sheet is still a stale sheet. IOMI's balance sheet updates itself.
- **Complete beats manual.** The sheet only knows what you typed. IOMI holds every asset class natively — including the ones banks can't see.
- **A next move beats a summary.** Copilot summarizes what you built. IOMI scores it, ranks what to do next, and lets you simulate the move first.
