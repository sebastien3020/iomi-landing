# IOMI vs Claude

> Markdown mirror of https://www.iomifinance.com/compare/claude. Last updated: 2026-08-24.

We like Claude — IOMI's own assistant runs on Anthropic's models. The difference isn't the model. It's what the model can see.

**The short version:** Claude on its own reasons over what you paste into the conversation. IOMI runs Claude-class intelligence on top of your live, complete balance sheet — with the math done by a deterministic engine, not the model. Same intelligence, radically different grounding.

## The comparison

| | IOMI | Claude |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A wealth platform where the data is already there, live, and structured | A general AI assistant — you bring the data to the conversation |
| Live accounts | Read-only sync with 12,000+ institutions via Plaid, plus every asset banks can't see | No bank connections — your numbers arrive by paste or upload, and age from that moment |
| Persistent balance sheet | Your balance sheet is the product: reconciled, historical, per-entity | Conversation memory — not an authoritative, reconciled source of truth for your wealth |
| The math | Computed by a deterministic engine; the AI phrases the numbers, it never invents them | Generated by the model — fluent, plausible, unverified |
| Proactive | A Daily Brief, goals tracked against real balances, flags on what changed | Answers when you ask |
| Privacy | The AI sees an aggregated financial snapshot only — never credentials, never account numbers | You paste financial details into a general-purpose conversation |

Claude capabilities as published by Anthropic, August 2026. IOMI's assistant is built on Anthropic's models — grounded in your live data.

## Why IOMI

- **The data problem is the product.** A model is only as good as what it sees. IOMI's entire job is making sure what it sees is your real, live, complete picture.
- **Computed, not generated.** Scores, forecasts and allocations come from a deterministic engine. The model explains; it never invents.
- **Nothing to paste, nothing to leak.** No copying statements into a chat window. The AI works from an aggregated snapshot — credentials and account numbers never reach it.
