# IOMI vs ChatGPT

> Markdown mirror of https://www.iomifinance.com/compare/chatgpt. Last updated: 2026-08-24.

ChatGPT connecting your banks was a real step. Here is exactly where it stops — and why that gap is the whole product.

**The short version:** ChatGPT with connected accounts is a general assistant with your bank feed. IOMI is a wealth platform with an assistant: your whole balance sheet — business entities, real estate, private positions, collectibles — computed by a deterministic engine and explained in plain English.

## The comparison

| | IOMI | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A wealth platform built around your balance sheet, with an AI assistant included | A general AI assistant that can also connect bank accounts |
| Data completeness | Your whole balance sheet: financial accounts plus real estate with live valuations, company equity, private positions, collectibles, vehicles, every liability | Bank and brokerage accounts through Plaid — no real estate, no company or private equity, no collectibles, no vehicles |
| Business + personal | Up to 3 business entities alongside personal wealth — separated, attributed, consolidated | One personal pile — no concept of an LLC or holding company |
| The math | Computed by a deterministic engine; the AI phrases the numbers, it never invents them | Insights generated by the model on your synced data — fluent, plausible, unverified |
| Proactive | A Daily Brief, goals tracked against real balances, flags on what changed since yesterday | Answers when you ask; memory for goals |
| Privacy | Single-purpose platform. Read-only via Plaid, credentials never seen, data never sold, never used to train general models | Transactions sync into a general-purpose AI provider's systems; synced data is deleted within 30 days after you disconnect |

ChatGPT personal finance capabilities as published by OpenAI, August 2026.

## Why IOMI

- **Your whole picture, not your bank feed.** Plaid-visible accounts are a slice. IOMI holds the rest: real estate, company equity, private positions, collectibles — the part of founder wealth a bank feed never returns.
- **Computed, not generated.** A score and a forecast you can act on have to come from an engine, not a language model. IOMI's AI explains the numbers; it is never their source.
- **Built for entities.** LLC, holding company, personal — separated and consolidated. A general assistant has no concept of which pocket a dollar sits in.
