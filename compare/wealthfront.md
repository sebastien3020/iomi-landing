# IOMI vs Wealthfront

> Markdown mirror of https://www.iomifinance.com/compare/wealthfront. Last updated: 2026-08-23.

Wealthfront and IOMI are different categories, not direct competitors. Wealthfront is an automated investment manager (robo-advisor): it custodies your money, invests it in diversified index portfolios for a 0.25% annual advisory fee on investment accounts, and offers a high-yield cash account. It does that job very well. IOMI is not in that category: it manages nothing and custodies nothing.

## What each one does

- **Wealthfront** manages the money you transfer to it. That is asset management — a real service, priced as a percentage of the assets it manages.
- **IOMI** is the intelligence layer over everything you own — bank, brokerage (including a Wealthfront account), real estate, crypto, company equity, collectibles, and business entities (up to 3, never mixed with personal). Connections are read-only via Plaid; IOMI can never move your money.

## The intelligence layer

- **Capital Efficiency Score (CES, 0–100)** — computed from five factors: liquidity, idle capital, drag, risk balance, concentration — tells you which factor to fix first.
- **Next Dollar Allocation** — where the next dollar does the most work.
- **Simulator** — model a reallocation or a planned cash-flow event on your own numbers before you commit; the engine recomputes deterministically.
- **Goals** and **IOMI AI** — answers from your real holdings.

## Pricing

- Wealthfront: 0.25% annual advisory fee on investment accounts; high-yield cash account.
- IOMI: flat subscription — Personal $29/month, Business $39/month founding rate ($79 regular), 1-month free trial, no credit card, 30-day money-back on the first paid month. Never a percentage of assets.

## Can you use both?

Yes — many people should. Wealthfront manages a slice of your money; IOMI shows you the whole picture, including that slice. If Wealthfront runs your index portfolio well, IOMI simply puts that portfolio next to the assets no manager sees and scores how hard the whole balance sheet is working.

## Who should pick which

Pick Wealthfront if you want your investing done for you, hands-off. Pick IOMI if your wealth is bigger than any one account and you want one score, one simulator and one AI over all of it — without handing anything over. Pick both if Wealthfront runs your portfolio and you still lack the whole picture.
