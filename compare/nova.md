# IOMI vs Nova Net Worth

> Markdown mirror of https://www.iomifinance.com/compare/nova. Last updated: 2026-08-22.

Nova Net Worth is the closest tool to IOMI by positioning: an "agentic balance sheet" for operators, founders and families, multi-entity from day one, with suggestions that cite the rows they come from. If you want a lean balance sheet with a few smart nudges at $15/month, it is a serious option.

## Where they differ

- **Composite score.** Nova proposes moves but has no single health metric. IOMI computes the Capital Efficiency Score (CES, 0–100) from five factors — liquidity, idle capital, drag, risk balance, concentration — and tells you which factor to fix first, plus the Next Dollar Allocation.
- **Simulator.** IOMI lets you model a reallocation or a planned cash-flow event on your own numbers before you commit; the engine recomputes your score, cash flow and projection deterministically.
- **Collectibles and physical assets.** Nova auto-values real estate, metals and crypto; IOMI also tracks watches, cars, wine, art and cards as stores of value in your net worth.
- **Entities.** Both are multi-entity. IOMI keeps business and personal side by side, never mixed, with a consolidated view (up to 3 entities on Business).
- **AI.** IOMI AI answers from your real holdings (100 messages/day Personal, 200/day Business) and never estimates when data is missing.

## Pricing

- Nova Net Worth: $15/month, single plan, 14-day trial.
- IOMI: Personal $29/month, Business $39/month founding rate ($79 regular), 1-month free trial, no credit card, 30-day money-back guarantee on the first paid month.

## Who should pick which

Pick Nova if you want the cheapest multi-entity balance sheet with row-citing suggestions. Pick IOMI if you want the score, the simulator and the planning layer on top of tracking — and collectibles in the picture.
