IOMI vs Monarch Money

Where each tool stops, and why that matters once you own more than a checking account.

Monarch Money is a well-built budgeting and net-worth app. If what you need is a shared budget with your partner and a clean monthly picture, it does that job.

The gap opens when your finances stop being a budget. A holding company, an LLC, real estate, a private equity position — that is a balance sheet, and a balance sheet raises a different question: not where did the money go, but what should the next dollar do. That is the question IOMI is built to answer.

Why IOMI

  • Business + personal, unified. Monarch handles business tracking on the surface, and only on the $199.99/yr Plus plan. IOMI treats entities as first-class: up to 3 business entities alongside your personal wealth, never mixed, from $39/mo.
  • Intelligence, not tracking. Monarch tells you what you have and where it went. IOMI adds the Capital Efficiency Score, Next Dollar Allocation and a simulator — a view of how hard your capital is working and what to change.
  • AI grounded in live data. The IOMI chat answers from your real, connected balance sheet, with the numbers computed rather than generated.
