    Why a subscription, not a percentage

    Wealth tools that charge a share of your assets charge you for growing. IOMI is a flat subscription: the price is the same whether your net worth is $200k or $20M, and there are no commissions, no product pushing, no hidden monetization. IOMI never custodies assets or executes trades — it is read-only by design.

    Common questions

    Is there a free trial? Yes — one month, full access, no credit card required, on both plans.

    Can I switch plans? Yes, anytime from Settings → Subscription. The Business founding rate stays locked while you remain subscribed.

    What happens if I cancel? You can delete your data and walk away anytime. No lock-in.

    Does IOMI work on my phone? Yes — it is a web app that works on any device with a browser, no app install required.
