## Strategic Metrics: Liquidity, Concentration, and the Capital Efficiency Score

Net worth is a vanity metric. It's a static number that looks impressive on a pitch deck but does nothing for your daily decision-making. ### Net Worth vs. Capital Efficiency

A $10M net worth can still feel like financial struggle if 95% of it is illiquid equity. This is the "paper rich, cash poor" trap that many high-growth founders fall into. To avoid this, you must measure the ROI of your personal capital against your business reinvestment rates. The IOMI Capital Efficiency Score (CES) is a 0–100 health check on how well your capital is deployed, computed deterministically from your real holdings on five factors: liquidity, idle capital, drag, risk balance and concentration. Each factor is scored out of 100, so it tells you which one to fix first. It turns a static snapshot into a live performance grade. High efficiency means your wealth is active, not just present.

### Managing Concentration and Tail Risk

Founder’s Bias is a dangerous blind spot. You believe in your vision, so you go "all-in." While this intensity builds companies, it also creates massive tail risk. True oversight means visualizing your business as a single line item in a broader, diversified portfolio. This isn't about a lack of faith in your company; it's about strategic resilience. By tracking personal vs business assets side-by-side, you can spot when your exposure to a single sector or entity becomes a threat to your personal lifestyle goals. You can't hedge what you don't track. Strategic diversification doesn't mean losing your edge. It means protecting the house so you can keep playing the game at the highest level.

The Liquidity Ratio is your "dry powder" metric. It ensures you have the agility to pivot when the market shifts. Whether it's a personal emergency or a sudden business acquisition opportunity, knowing your real-time liquidity across all entities is vital. Consider these tactical focal points:

  * **Cash-to-Debt Ratio:** Monitor your leverage across all LLCs to prevent cross-entity contagion.
  * **Burn Runway:** Calculate how long your liquid personal assets can support your lifestyle without business distributions.
  * **Exit Readiness:** Evaluate the time-to-liquidity for alternative assets like physical collectibles or private equity.



Total oversight gives you the permission to be bold. When you understand your concentration and liquidity at a granular level, you move from a defensive posture to an offensive one. Confidence comes from the data. Control comes from the system.
