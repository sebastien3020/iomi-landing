Understanding the Components of Your Score

Your CES isn't a random number. This metric is a sophisticated calculation of your strategic advantage. IOMI evaluates three critical pillars:

  • Liquidity vs. Concentration: High net worth doesn't equal financial health if it's all locked in illiquid private equity. Over-concentration in a single sector lowers your score.
  • Idle Capital: This is the silent killer of wealth. IOMI spots cash sitting in low-yield bank accounts. It uses an illustrative $180,000 idle capital anchor to demonstrate the real cost of inaction.
  • Risk Balance: The system looks across your business entities and personal holdings. It spots exposure gaps you miss in a manual spreadsheet.

The platform's logic is self-evident. It identifies the friction in your portfolio and provides a clear path to resolution. You move from a state of disorder to total oversight with one unified score.
