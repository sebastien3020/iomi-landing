Understanding the Capital Efficiency Score (CES)

The CES is a deterministic health check for your entire portfolio. It distills complex data into a single 0-100 metric, providing an objective assistant that never sleeps. This score turns abstract data into a competitive benchmark by analyzing four critical pillars:

  • Liquidity: Ensures you have the right amount of dry powder without over-allocating to low-yield cash.
  • Idle Capital: Identifies funds sitting in unoptimized accounts that aren't compounding.
  • Risk Balance: Measures your exposure across different asset classes to prevent over-leveraging.
  • Concentration: Flags when too much wealth is tied to a single LLC or brokerage entry.

High-achievers use the CES to spot drag before it stalls growth. It moves you from a state of disorder to one of total oversight. If your score is low, the system doesn't just flag it; it explains why. It's a sharp, no-nonsense approach to results that keeps you ahead of the curve.
